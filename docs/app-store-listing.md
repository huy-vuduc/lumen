Description:

```
Lumen is a quiet puzzle about light.

Each chamber hides a few beams in the fog. Turn the mirrors so every beam finds the ring of its own colour, and the room lights up.

There is no clock and no score. Only a handful of rotations per chamber, so every turn is a choice.

TWELVE CHAPTERS, ONE NEW IDEA EACH
• Mirrors: bend a beam around the room
• Walls: stone that swallows light
• Mixing: red and blue meet on one ring and turn magenta
• Fixed mirrors: some can't be turned, plan around them
• Splitters: one beam becomes two
• Dark rings: keep the light away
• Filters: a beam keeps only one colour
• Portals: light leaves one door and enters another
• Then everything together, up to 9 x 9 boards

120 CHAMBERS
Ten per chapter. Early chapters teach; from chapter two every chamber is a real puzzle, and the spare rotation disappears as you go.

MADE TO BE CALM
Soft colours, slow beams, fog that clears as the light passes. Play in English or Vietnamese.

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL ADS
Each chamber gives you two retries, each chapter three hints that show one mirror. Out of either? Watch a short ad, or wait: a retry comes back every 30 minutes. No ad is ever shown unless you ask for it.
```

What's New (1.0): `First light. Twelve chapters, 120 chambers, English and Vietnamese.`

Review notes:

```
Lumen is a single-player puzzle game. No account or login is needed; everything is stored on the device.
Ads are rewarded videos only (Google AdMob) and appear only when the player taps "Watch an ad" for an extra hint or retry. The tracking permission prompt appears before the first ad request.
To see later chapters quickly: Settings has no unlock switch in this build; chapters unlock after finishing the previous one. Chapter 1 chambers take under a minute each.
```
